# Marketplace Sales Analysis — Power BI

# Project Overview
This project analyzes sales behavior in an international marketplace between 2020 and 2024 using Power BI. The dataset includes transactions, users, products, and companies, allowing the exploration of business performance, geographic purchasing patterns, customer segmentation, and product profitability.
The analysis was developed through an exploratory and analytical approach focused on identifying trends, anomalies, and possible causes behind the sales decline observed in the last two years.

## Objectives
•	Analyze sales evolution over the last five years 
•	Identify seasonal patterns and sales trends 
•	Compare buyer and company geographic distribution 
•	Explore international vs local purchasing behavior 
•	Analyze customer segments and product performance 
•	Investigate possible causes behind the 2023–2024 sales decline 

## Tools & Technologies
•	Power BI 
•	DAX 
•	Power Query 
•	SQL 
•	Data visualization 
•	Business analytics 

## Key Insights
•	Sales grew until 2022, followed by two consecutive years below the business target. 
•	International purchasing dominates across almost all markets. 
•	Adults aged 40–65 consistently generate the highest share of revenue. 
•	Some products generate high sales volume but comparatively low revenue due to low pricing. 
•	Effective transactions decreased steadily from 2021 to 2024 while rejected transactions increased. 

## Dataset Structure
The data follows a star schema model.
•	Fact table: transactions 
•	Dimensions: 
o	users 
o	products 
o	companies 
o	credit cards
